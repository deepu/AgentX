---
title: Review the binding
performer: person
assignee: manager
strictness: strict
reads: [case.plugin, case.version, case.summary, case.report, case.draft]
outcomes: [approved, changes_requested]
---
Read what the plugin can reach and how it binds: each tool with its class and where the class
came from (declared, MCP hints, the binder agent, a person), the capabilities it uses, how its
entities and fields map onto the domain's, its record mappings with sample records before and
after, and the dry-run report. Approve it to freeze it as the next binding version, or ask for
changes and say what: your comment goes back to staff.
