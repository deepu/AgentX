How it works

From a conversation to a working process.

You don't need a developer to get started. Everything is set up by talking, and nothing goes live until you've said yes.

Step 1

Describe it

Explain the process the way you would to a new hire, or upload the checklist you already use. Start from a template if you like.

Step 2

Shape it

See your process as steps you recognise. Say who does each one, what needs a sign-off, and what should never happen without you.

Step 3

Connect your tools

Email, spreadsheets, your CRM, your accounting system. MemWise suggests how your records line up with them, and you confirm.

Step 4

Try it, then go live

Run it on a few example cases first and see exactly what would happen. Switch it on when you're happy.

You stay in charge

Hand over the busywork, not the judgement.

You set what agents may do, in plain terms. MemWise holds them to it on every step, even when they're unsure or reading something they shouldn't trust.

Decide what agents may do

Every action an agent can take is sorted by what it does. You pick the rule for each kind.

Look things up Find a client, read a document Always fine
Prepare Draft an email or a letter Always fine
Update records Change a client's details Only its own step
Contact people Send an email to a client Ask me first
Can't be undone Make a payment Never

Ask any step why

Every step keeps a record of what it looked at and why it decided. Ask it, and correct it if it got something wrong.

Why was Northwind flagged?
The form says the director is Dana Okafor, but the certificate they uploaded names Dana Okoro. Everything else matched.

Nothing falls through the cracks

A process can wait days for a client or a colleague and pick up exactly where it left off. Nothing is done twice, and nothing is forgotten.
