How the factory works

Every playbook becomes a production line.

Work comes in, agents do the steps, your team checks the result, and finished work goes out. Every case, the same way, every day.

Playbooks are the lines

Each line is a playbook in plain English: the steps, who does each one, and what good looks like. Change a sentence and the next case follows it.

Agents are the workers

They read the documents, fill in the forms, check the registers and chase people. They never get bored of the hundredth case.

Each line gets its screens

Client forms, record pages, review screens and a live board come with the playbook. Nothing to design, nothing to build.

Your team is quality control

People sign off where it matters. Nothing leaves the line without the checks you set, and every decision is on record.

Your first line runs this afternoon.

No developer, no IT project. Nothing goes live until you've said yes.
