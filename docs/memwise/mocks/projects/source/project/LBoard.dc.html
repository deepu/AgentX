Weight loss

Oral GLP-1s vs placebo · 5 phase 3 trials
↻ Refreshes every MondayLast: Mon 1 June · accepted by Leah

Oral GLP-1s gave 9 to 13 points more weight loss than placebo over 64 to 72 weeks. In people with type 2 diabetes, 7 points. After switching from injections, most of the weight lost stayed off.
