How often do people stop oral GLP-1s because of side effects?
✓ Searched PubMed: 13 randomised trials of oral GLP-1s for weight since 2023✓ Read the abstracts of the 5 phase 3 trials

In the orforglipron trials, 5 to 10% stopped because of side effects, against 3 to 4% on placebo. In OASIS 2, 4.5% stopped on oral semaglutide 50 mg.

The other oral semaglutide abstracts don't give this number. They report stomach and gut side effects instead: 80% against 46% on placebo in OASIS 1.

I put it on a page on the right, as a draft under GLP-1. To fill the gaps I can read the full papers.
