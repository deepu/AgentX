To: Dr Ruth Adeyemi · Re: oral semaglutide 25 mg vs the weekly injection
Dear Dr Adeyemi, No trial has compared oral semaglutide 25 mg with the 2.4 mg weekly injection directly. In OASIS 4, people taking 25 mg lost 13.6% of their body weight over 64 weeks, against 2.2% on placebo [1]. A comparison with the STEP 1 trial of the injection found similar weight loss (difference 1.0 percentage points, 95% CI −1.6 to 3.6) [2]. This comparison is indirect, across two separate trials, and should be read with caution. [1] Wharton S et al. N Engl J Med 2025. doi:10.1056/NEJMoa2500969
[2] Plotkin M et al. Diabetes Obes Metab 2026. doi:10.1111/dom.70911
